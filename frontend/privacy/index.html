Privacy Policy

This page explains what information Nexauren may process, why it is needed, how it can be used, and the choices available to you.

1. Privacy at a glance

Nexauren is a digital tools platform. Information may be processed to create and secure accounts, provide requested tools, operate the website, manage plans and credits, process payments, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.

Important: This policy is intended to describe the service as it operates. It may be updated when new tools, providers or processing activities are introduced.

This policy is written in plain language where possible. It is informational and does not replace legal advice about your specific situation.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • create, authenticate and manage your account;
  • provide the tools and features you request;
  • process and verify plans, credits and purchases;
  • protect the platform from fraud, abuse and unauthorized access;
  • diagnose errors and improve performance and reliability;
  • respond to support or account requests; and
  • meet legal, security or operational obligations.

Nexauren should not use information for a materially incompatible purpose without an appropriate legal basis or notice where required.

4. Files and tool processing

Some tools require your file, text or other input to be processed by Nexauren's backend or by a service provider used to perform that tool's function. Processing can therefore vary by tool.

Before submitting sensitive material, check the tool's description and instructions. Unless a specific feature explicitly says otherwise, do not treat Nexauren as a secure archive or permanent file-storage service.

AI-assisted tools can process submitted content to generate a requested result. The result may be imperfect, and you should review important outputs.

Good practice: submit only the minimum information necessary for the task and keep your own copy of important files.

5. Payments and third-party payment providers

When you buy a plan or credits, payment processing is handled through the payment provider presented during checkout. Nexauren may receive transaction-related information such as purchase status, product identifiers and references needed to grant the purchased benefit.

Payment providers may independently process payment information under their own privacy notices and terms. Do not place complete payment-card details into ordinary Nexauren forms or tool inputs.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

Nexauren may use essential cookies or similar mechanisms to support sessions, authentication, security and basic site functionality. Other technologies may be used only where the relevant feature and applicable law permit them.

See the dedicated Cookies Policy for more information about categories and choices.

7. When information may be shared

Information may be disclosed when necessary to operate Nexauren, such as to infrastructure, authentication, AI/tool-processing, analytics or payment providers that support a requested feature. Providers should receive information appropriate to their role and applicable arrangements.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect rights and safety, investigate abuse, or support a corporate transaction such as a merger, acquisition or transfer of assets.

Nexauren does not promise that every tool uses the same providers. The relevant tool's technical operation and this policy should be considered together.

8. Security

Nexauren uses technical and organizational measures intended to protect accounts and service data. Authentication and session controls are part of the platform's security architecture.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Use a unique password, protect your device, avoid sharing account access, and report suspected unauthorized access through the available support/contact method.

9. Retention

Information may be kept for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account and transaction records, protect security, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, or fulfil the purpose for which it was collected.

Retention can differ by information type and feature. When information is no longer needed for a legitimate purpose, it should be deleted or otherwise handled according to applicable legal and operational requirements.

10. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on where you live and which privacy laws apply, you may have rights such as access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing, data portability, or withdrawal of consent where consent is the legal basis.

To make a privacy request, use the contact/support method made available by Nexauren and provide enough information for the request to be understood and verified. Nexauren may need to verify a request before acting on it and may have lawful reasons to retain certain information.

If you are in a jurisdiction with a right to complain to a data-protection authority, you may also contact the authority responsible for your location.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

As Nexauren grows, new tools, payment methods, analytics, security controls or other services may change the information we process. When that happens, this policy may be updated and the date at the top of the page will change.

For significant changes, Nexauren will aim to provide additional notice when appropriate.
