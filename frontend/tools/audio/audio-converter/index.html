Online audio to WAV converter

Nexauren Audio Converter is a free browser-based tool for converting audio that your browser can decode into standard 16-bit PCM WAV. It is useful for music production, voice recordings, samples and everyday audio files.

How to convert audio to WAV

  1. Choose an audio file or drag it into the converter.
  2. Preview the source audio.
  3. Select WAV as the output format.
  4. Start conversion and download the WAV file.

Frequently asked questions

Is Nexauren Audio Converter free?

Yes. Audio Converter is completely free and does not use Nexauren credits.

Does it use FFmpeg?

No. The rebuilt converter does not load FFmpeg or another conversion engine. It uses the browser's native audio decoder and a WAV encoder written for Nexauren.

Which formats can I convert?

Input support depends on the browser's native audio decoder. WAV is the output format because WAV can be created directly without an external codec engine.

Are my audio files uploaded?

No. The conversion is performed locally in your browser. Nexauren does not need to receive the selected audio file.
