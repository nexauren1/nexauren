Image Background Remover

Remove a plain or solid-color background from an image and create a transparent PNG directly in your browser.

Transparent areas are shown with a checkerboard.
Higher values remove more colors similar to the selected background.

How it works

Upload an image, choose the background color or sample it from a corner, then adjust tolerance and edge softness. Everything runs locally in your browser.
