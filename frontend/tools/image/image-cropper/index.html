How to use

  1. Select or drop an image.
  2. Move the crop frame or drag a corner to choose the exact area.
  3. Optionally choose a fixed aspect ratio and output format.
  4. Click Crop image and download the result.

Your image is processed locally in your browser. It is not uploaded to a Nexauren server.
