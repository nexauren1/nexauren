Image Compressor FAQ

Is this tool really free?

Yes. Nexauren Image Compressor is free and does not use credits or require a plan.

Are my images uploaded?

No upload is required for the compression itself. Processing is performed locally in your browser.

Which format should I use?

WebP is usually a good choice for smaller files. JPEG is useful for broad compatibility. PNG is best when you need lossless output or transparency.
