Help

Frequently asked questions.

What is Nexauren?

Nexauren is a digital toolkit with practical tools for audio, images, PDFs, text, AI and other tasks.

Do I need an account?

Some features can be used without an account, while account-based features use your Nexauren account.

Where can I find the tools?

Open the Tools directory to browse the available tools and categories.

How do plans and credits work?

Available plans and credit options are shown on the Plans page before you choose to purchase.

How can I get help?

Check the information on the relevant tool or account page for the feature you are using.
